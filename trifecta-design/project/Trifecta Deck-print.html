TRIFECTAPart of Midpoint Global /Avoiding two costly errors 05
Two costly errors

The mistakes a real MMM stops you making.

Most growth losses live in one of these two buckets. Trifecta is engineered to spot both.

−
Errors of commission

Spending on channels that don't drive the business.

Money wasted where we should not have spent. Last-click attribution loves these channels. The geo-holdout test exposes them. Trifecta surfaces them inside the operator console so they can be defunded with conviction.
Typical signature
High reported ROAS · near-zero incremental lift in regional test.
+
Errors of omission

Failing to invest in channels that do drive the business.

Money never spent where we should have spent it. Often the slowest-burning, hardest-to-attribute channels like upper-funnel video, OOH, and podcast. They compound but never close a session.
Typical signature
Low last-click share · positive marginal ROI past current spend.

TRIFECTAPart of Midpoint Global /Delivery Lifecycle 10
Delivery lifecycle

Design. Build. Operate. Transfer.

Five stages, repeated per client. The first run takes weeks. Subsequent runs are an operator job, not a project.

01
GROWTH & DATA AUDIT

Audit the spend, audit the data.

We assess current marketing investments and evaluate every data stream natively available in BigQuery, mapping the external and control variables we'll need.

02
DATA INGESTION

Capture, clean, inject.

We pull and harmonise every input, API-available or not, into BigQuery. Signal is separated from noise with a per-source mapping you can re-use forever.

03
MODEL CALIBRATION

Anchor the model to measured lift.

We continually run geo-holdout incrementality experiments and inject their results into the model as deterministic priors.

04
HYPOTHESIS FORMULATION

Decide what to test next.

We mine Ads Data Hub DDA data for the hypotheses worth testing. Every experiment then closes a loop the model needs closed.

05
DEMOCRATISATION

Put the model in everyone's chat.

We connect Meridian to an MCP server so anyone in your organisation can query the MMM in plain language, then act on what it says.

TRIFECTAPart of Midpoint Global /Poor MMM Practices 12
Poor MMM practices

Five anti-patterns we refuse to repeat.

If you've tried MMM before and it didn't stick, one of these is usually why.

ANTI-PATTERN 01

Annual planning cycles

Engaging MMM once a year. The world is dynamic. The planning cycle must be quarters or weeks, not seasons.

ANTI-PATTERN 02

Post-report abandonment

Letting data ingestion and Bayesian priors decay after the consultant report ships. A static MMM is a dead MMM.

ANTI-PATTERN 03

Irregular prior updates

Not feeding priors systematically keeps models unintelligent. Regular updates compound into real knowledge.

ANTI-PATTERN 04

Ignoring Ads Data Hub

Overlooking the only privacy-safe clean-room for cross-device customer journey insights, out of the box and at scale.

ANTI-PATTERN 05

Lack of democratisation

MMM has to be accessible via conversational AI. Without that, it never becomes part of the company's growth DNA.
